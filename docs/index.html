Gratitude – Daily Journal ✦

A few lines a day about what went well. Simple enough to keep going even on your hardest days.

Get it on Google Play
  • Up to ten things you're grateful for each day, with photos and a voice note
  • A calendar, mood tracking and stats that show how your practice grows
  • Works offline and backs up to the cloud automatically. Add your email to restore your entries on a new phone
  • Seven languages, light and dark theme, fingerprint lock

Every feature is free. The free version shows ads, and an optional annual subscription removes them. We never use your journal entries for advertising.
