### 2. Premiera po angielsku

> I built and shipped my first mobile app – after hours, on my own. 📱
>
> "Gratitude – Daily Journal" is a simple gratitude journal for Android. I took it from idea to Google Play myself: design, code, 7 languages, a 14-day closed test with 12 testers and Google's review.
>
> What I learned:
> → The hardest part wasn't the code, it was the store requirements.
> → Testers find things you won't see after 100 launches.
> → Keeping it simple takes more work than adding features.
>
> If you keep a journal (or want to start), I'd be grateful – pun intended – for your feedback. Link in the first comment.
>
